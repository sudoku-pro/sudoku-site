Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for iPhone, iPad & Mac players

Is Sudoku free?
Check the App Store listing for current pricing. The app is a one-time purchase with no ads or in-app purchases.
Does Sudoku work offline?
Yes. The game is fully playable offline — no internet needed for gameplay. CloudKit sync and Game Center require internet when active, but core features work 100% offline.
Is my game data synced?
Yes! Your settings, statistics, and game saves sync via iCloud (CloudKit) across all your Apple devices. Start on iPhone, continue on Mac.
Which devices are supported?
Sudoku is a universal app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac with native controls and Retina graphics. Requires iOS/iPadOS 16.0+ and macOS 15.0 (Sequoia)+. One purchase works everywhere.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Sudoku is completely ad-free with no pop-ups or in-app purchases. Just pure, focused puzzle gameplay.
