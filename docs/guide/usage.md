# Usage Guide

## Basic Gameplay

### Objective

Fill a 9×9 grid so that every **row**, every **column**, and each of the nine 3×3 **boxes** contains all digits from 1 to 9 exactly once. No repetition allowed in any unit.

### How to Play

- **Place a number**: Tap or click an empty cell, then tap a digit from the number pad at the bottom of the screen.
- **Pencil notes (candidates)**: Toggle Pencil mode, then tap cells to write small candidate digits. Useful for tracking possibilities.
- **Erase**: Tap the Erase button to clear a cell's value (or pencil marks when in pencil mode).
- **Hint**: Tap Hint to reveal one correct cell's value.
- **Undo**: Tap Undo to reverse your last move (supports full undo history).

### Difficulty Levels

Each difficulty level determines how many cells are pre-filled (given) at the start:

| Level | Given Cells | Best For |
|-------|------------|----------|
| Easy | 45 | Beginners & quick games |
| Medium | 36 | Casual players |
| Hard | 31 | Regular solvers |
| Expert | 27 | Experienced players |
| Master | 24 | Puzzle masters |

## Game Controls

### iPhone / iPad

- **Tap** an empty cell to select it
- **Tap a number** on the keypad to place it
- **Toggle Pencil mode** to enter/erase candidate notes
- **Use Erase** to clear a cell
- **Use Hint** for help when stuck
- **Use Undo** to take back moves

### Mac

- **Left-click** an empty cell to select it
- **Type a number key (1–9)** or click the on-screen keypad to place
- **Toggle Pencil mode** for candidate notes
- **Keyboard shortcuts**:
  - `⌘N` — New game
  - `⌘Z` — Undo
  - `⌘1` through `⌘5` — Start new game at each difficulty level

## Smart Features

### Pencil Notes System

When you enable Pencil mode, you can write small candidate digits in empty cells. The smart pencil system:

- **Auto-clears peer pencil marks**: When you place a definitive number, pencil marks for that digit are automatically removed from all cells in the same row, column, and box.
- **Toggle per cell**: Switch between entry mode and pencil mode at any time.

### Error Checking

Optional error highlighting shows incorrect entries in red, helping you learn without spoiling the puzzle.

### Daily Challenge

A fresh Master-level puzzle is generated every day. Your daily challenge progress is tracked in Statistics with a calendar view showing your streak.

### Statistics

Track your Sudoku journey:

- Total games played and win rate
- Current streak and best streak
- Per-difficulty breakdown: games won, best time, average time
- Daily Challenge calendar with completion indicators

## iCloud Sync

When signed into iCloud on your device:

- Game saves sync automatically — continue on any device
- Statistics and settings stay in sync
- Daily Challenge progress transfers across devices
- Requires iOS/iPadOS 16+ or macOS 15+

## Game Center

Connect with Game Center to:

- Compete on **leaderboards** for best completion times per difficulty
- Earn **achievements**: First Solve, Easy/Medium/Hard/Expert/Master Clear, Flawless (no hints or errors)

## Print Puzzles (Mac)

On Mac, print puzzles directly from the app:

1. Open the **Print** dialog from the toolbar
2. Select difficulty level (Easy through Master)
3. Toggle "Include Answers" if desired
4. Click **Print**

Perfect for solving offline with pen and paper!

## Sound Effects

Sudoku includes subtle sound effects:

- Number placement confirmation
- Hint revealed
- Error alert
- Victory celebration

Toggle sound on or off in **Settings → Sound**.

## Tips and Tricks

- **Start with what's given** — scan rows, columns, and boxes that already have many numbers filled.
- **Use pencil notes** — they're especially helpful on Hard, Expert, and Master difficulties.
- **Look for naked singles** — if a cell has only one possible candidate, fill it in.
- **Look for hidden singles** — if a digit can only go in one place within a row/column/box, place it there.
- **Use hints sparingly** — they're great for learning, but completing a puzzle without hints feels more rewarding.
- **Build your daily streak** — the Daily Challenge is a great way to practice consistently.
