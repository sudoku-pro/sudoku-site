# Installation

Sudoku is available as a universal app on the App Store, supporting iPhone, iPad, and Mac with a single purchase.

## Download

- **App Store**: [Download Sudoku](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793606018) — universal app for iPhone, iPad & Mac

### App Store (Recommended)

The App Store version provides automatic updates and seamless integration across all your Apple devices.

- **App Store ID**: 6793606018
- **Link**: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793606018
- **Platforms**: iPhone, iPad, Mac (universal app)
- **Price**: Check the App Store listing for current pricing

## Installation Steps

### iPhone / iPad

1. Open the **App Store** on your device
2. Search for "Sudoku" or use the direct link above
3. Tap **Get** or **Buy** to download
4. The app will install automatically
5. Launch Sudoku from your Home Screen

### Mac

1. Open the **App Store** on your Mac
2. Search for "Sudoku" or use the direct link above
3. Click **Get** or **Buy** to purchase
4. The app will automatically download and install
5. Launch Sudoku from your Applications folder or Launchpad

## First Launch

When you launch Sudoku for the first time, it will request permission to access iCloud (for sync) and Game Center (for leaderboards and achievements). These are optional — the game works fully offline without them.

## Updates

Updates are handled automatically by the App Store. To check for updates manually, open the App Store and go to your **Account → Updates** section.

## Uninstall

### iPhone / iPad

1. Press and hold the Sudoku app icon
2. Tap **Remove App** → **Delete App**

### Mac

1. Quit Sudoku
2. Drag `Sudoku.app` from `Applications` to the Trash
3. (Optional) Remove app data:

```bash
rm -rf ~/Library/Containers/com.w3cub.sudoku
```

## System Requirements

- **iPhone/iPad**: iOS/iPadOS 16.0 or later
- **Mac**: macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or later
- **CPU**: Apple Silicon (M-series)
- **Memory**: 2GB minimum
- **Storage**: ~50 MB
- **Network**: Not required for gameplay (iCloud/Game Center optional)

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues during installation:

- **macOS blocks the app**: Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** for Sudoku
- **App not appearing**: Restart the App Store app or your device and try again

For more help, visit the [Troubleshooting guide](./troubleshooting) or open an issue on the project repository.
