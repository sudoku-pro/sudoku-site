# Introduction

## About Sudoku

**Sudoku — The Classic Number Puzzle, Perfectly Made.**

![Sudoku running on iPad](/screenshot-ipad-main.png)

Rediscover the timeless logic puzzle you love, refined for modern devices with smooth controls, beautiful themes, and endless replayability. Whether you are a first-time player or a seasoned expert, this is the Sudoku experience you have been waiting for.

## Core Features

### 🔢 **Authentic Gameplay**
True to the classic rules — fill a 9×9 grid so that every row, column, and 3×3 box contains digits 1 through 9 exactly once. Tap to place numbers, use pencil notes for candidates, and let logic guide you to the solution.

### 🎯 **5 Difficulty Levels**
Five carefully calibrated difficulties with different numbers of given cells:

| Level | Given Cells | Description |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| Easy | 45 | Great for beginners and quick games |
| Medium | 36 | A balanced challenge for casual players |
| Hard | 31 | Requires logical deduction techniques |
| Expert | 27 | For experienced solvers who want a real test |
| Master | 24 | The ultimate challenge — only for the bravest |

Each puzzle is uniquely generated — no two games are ever the same.

### 📱 **Universal App**
One purchase works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Native controls for each platform — touch gestures on iOS, mouse and keyboard on Mac. Your progress syncs via iCloud across all your devices.

![Sudoku on iPhone](/screenshot-iphone-main.png)

![Sudoku on Mac](/screenshot-mac-difficulty.png)

### 💡 **Hints & Notes**
- **Hint**: Stuck? Get a hint that reveals one correct cell without spoiling the whole puzzle.
- **Pencil (Notes)**: Toggle pencil mode to jot down candidate digits in empty cells. Pencil marks in peer cells (same row/column/box) auto-clear when you place a number.
- **Error Checking**: Optional highlighting of incorrect entries so you can learn from mistakes.
- **Undo**: Full undo support — take back any move, anytime.

### 🏆 **Daily Challenge**
A fresh puzzle every day at the Master difficulty level. Build a streak and track your daily progress on the calendar view in Statistics.

### 📊 **Statistics**
Track your performance: total games played, win rate, current streak, best streak, and per-difficulty breakdowns including best times and averages. Synced via iCloud.

### 🎨 **8 Beautiful Themes**
Personalize your board with eight handcrafted color themes: Classic, Midnight, Paper, Forest, Ocean, Sunset, Lavender, and Rose. Each theme provides a distinct visual experience while maintaining excellent readability.

### ☁️ **iCloud Sync**
Your settings, statistics, game saves, and daily challenge progress automatically sync across all your Apple devices via CloudKit. Start a game on your iPhone, continue on your Mac.

### 🎮 **Game Center**
Compete on leaderboards with your best completion times, and earn achievements: First Solve, Easy Clear, Medium Clear, Hard Clear, Expert Clear, Master Clear, and Flawless (complete without hints or errors).

### 🔇 **Clean & Ad-Free**
No interruptions, no pop-ups, no ads, no in-app purchases. Just pure, focused puzzle gameplay.

### 🌍 **25 Languages**
Switch languages at runtime from Settings without restarting the app. Supports English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Portuguese, Dutch, Vietnamese, Thai, Hindi, Turkish, Greek, Finnish, Norwegian, Danish, Hungarian, Indonesian, Malay, Ukrainian, and German (Switzerland).

### 🔊 **Sound Effects**
Subtle sound effects for number placement, hints, errors, and victory — toggle on or off in Settings.

### 🖨️ **Print Puzzles**
Print puzzles directly from the Mac app with difficulty selection and optional answer keys. Perfect for offline solving with pen and paper.

## System Requirements

- **iPhone/iPad**: iOS/iPadOS 16.0 or later
- **Mac**: macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or later (Apple Silicon)
- **Storage**: ~50 MB

## Privacy

Sudoku is offline-first for core gameplay. All game data is stored locally and synced to your personal iCloud account only. No ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking. The only network features are iCloud sync, Game Center, and an optional "More Apps" section in Settings.

See the [Privacy Policy](/guide/privacy) for details.
